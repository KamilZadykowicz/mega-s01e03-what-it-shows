The desk counts 379 under the parser: it files every #4471 report that is not a booking-v3 form report there, by tenant. Five of those are the other problems above, split out as tickets 3–5. Unique reports in #4471: 412.

1 · The parser flip — 379 reports

Every tenant we take reports for writes the day first (en-GB, de-DE, es-ES, nl-NL, cs-CZ, it-IT, fr-FR, pl-PL, pt-PT, da-DK, en-IE). The shared parser reads every date month first, so a tenant's dates come out flipped: "5 August" becomes 8 May. This is what most of #4471 is, and the fix already queued covers it. First seen 2026-04-01, last 2026-10-04, all 11 tenants, channels app/email/form.

Four of these name the problem without a date pair in the body: a feature request for a day-first setting (R-41290), two wrong-day complaints (R-41417, R-41514), and October bookings arriving as January (R-41615 — 1 October parsed as 10 January). We file them here because each is this bug as its reporter lived it.

2 · The booking-v3 hint — 33 reports

Through the booking-v3 form the hint under the date field says MM/DD/YYYY, so the tenant's people type the month first — and the form then reads it the tenant's way, day first. The dates flip too, which is why these landed in #4471, but the cause is the hint, not the parser: fixing the parser will not touch this form. All 33 reports came through the form channel from the three booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio 12, copper.studio 11, pale.harbour 10). The fix is the hint text.

3 · One day off — 3 reports

R-41202 (invite says Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May), R-41209 ("every booking in June is one day off. Only June."), R-41620 (Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday). Three tenants, three channels, no date flip — the booking moves by exactly one day. The "only June" report points at DST handling in day boundary math. Intake tagged all three parser; none of them is.

4 · The invite that came before the booking — 1 report

R-41236 — the invite arrived before the reporter made the booking. Not a date parse at all: the invite timestamp is wrong, or the invite is sent on a clock ahead of the booking. One report, nine.lanterns, app channel.

5 · Two days for one meeting — 1 report

R-41344 — a client in Lisbon got Tuesday, a client in Toronto got Monday, same meeting, held twice. The invite renders the day in the sender's zone instead of the recipient's. One report, pale.harbour, email channel.

What the desk read differently

desk-notes.json read five reports as one "one day move": R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620. They are three problems — one day off (3), the early invite (1), the two-zone meeting (1) — and none of them is the parser.
